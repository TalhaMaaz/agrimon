For your FYP Mid-Semester Presentation, here are General Presentation Guidelines
* Your presentation must consist of 10–12 slides.
* Each group will have strictly 10 minutes for the presentation.
* We have limited time and multiple projects to evaluate, so please strictly adhere to the allocated time.
* It is strongly recommended that you practice your presentation beforehand to ensure that you can comfortably complete it within 10 minutes.
* Please also bring a copy of your attendance sheet / FYP progress page.
* Your presentation must reflect the discussions, activities, and topics mentioned in your weekly attendance / progress sheet. There should be consistency between your documented progress and what you present.
* Attendance of all students is mandatory during all presentations, which may be verified by calling roll at multiple random intervals during activity.
